COLORING

CAN I COLOR MY EXTENSIONS?

You can darken them, but lightening damages the hair permanently.

THE SHORT ANSWER

Human hair extensions can be colored darker or toned with semi-permanent color. This is relatively safe when done by a professional.

Lightening extensions with bleach is not recommended. Extension hair has already been processed during manufacturing, and further bleaching weakens the cuticle and causes irreversible damage.

It's better to order extensions in the right color from the start than to try to correct them with color later.

✦

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Darken extensions. Adding lowlights or going a few shades darker is safe and can help blend extensions with color-treated natural hair.

Tone or gloss. Semi-permanent color can adjust undertones or add dimension without damaging the hair. Useful for matching your natural color more closely.

Root shadowing. Darkening the roots of extensions helps them blend seamlessly with your natural hair, especially if you have darker roots.

✦

WHAT TO AVOID

Bleaching or lightening. Extension hair can't repair itself like natural hair. Lightening strips the cuticle, leading to dryness, tangling, and a short lifespan.

DIY color jobs. Extensions process differently than natural hair. A professional colorist who works with extensions regularly will get better, safer results.

Frequent coloring. Even deposit-only color can dry out extensions over time. Limit color treatments and use quality, sulfate-free aftercare products.

✦

MATCHING YOUR NATURAL COLOR

The best approach is to order extensions that closely match your natural or desired hair color from the beginning.

If you regularly color your natural hair, coordinate timing so your roots and extensions are refreshed together. This keeps everything cohesive.

Slight color variations can actually look natural — perfectly matched hair can appear flat. A mix of tones creates dimension and movement.
