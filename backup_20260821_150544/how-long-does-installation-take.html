TIME COMMITMENT

HOW LONG DOES INSTALLATION TAKE?

From 1 to 5 hours, depending on the method and amount of hair.

BY EXTENSION TYPE

Tape-in extensions: 1-2 hours. The sandwiched panels attach quickly, making this the fastest installation method. Great if you're short on time.

Hand-sewn wefts: 2-3 hours. Creating beaded rows and sewing wefts takes more time than tape-in but less than keratin bonds.

Keratin bond extensions: 3-5 hours or more. Each individual strand is applied one at a time, which is meticulous and time-consuming. Worth it for the natural result.

WHAT AFFECTS INSTALLATION TIME

Amount of hair. A full head of extensions takes longer than partial volume or length. More rows or bonds = more time.

Hair texture and thickness. Working with thick, textured hair can take longer because proper sectioning and blending require more attention.

Customization. If you want custom color blending, root shadowing, or specific placement, expect additional time.

Experience level. A specialist who focuses on extensions works faster and more efficiently than someone who does them occasionally.

WHAT TO EXPECT DURING INSTALLATION

Plan to be in the chair for the full appointment. Extensions can't be rushed — proper placement prevents damage and ensures they look natural.

Bring headphones, a book, or something to do if you're getting keratin bonds. The process is methodical and takes focus.

Your stylist will section, prep, and attach the extensions, then blend and style everything so you leave looking polished.

MOVE-UP APPOINTMENTS

Move-up appointments for hand-sewn weft and tape-in extensions are faster than initial installations — usually 1-2 hours.

The hair is already prepped and you're reusing it, so the process is just removing, repositioning, and reattaching.

Keratin bond removal and reinstallation can take as long as the original installation, but you're typically only doing this every 4-6 months.
