WHAT ARE HAND-SEWN WEFT EXTENSIONS?

Hand-sewn weft extensions consist of wefts (horizontal hair tracks) that are hand-sewn onto rows of tiny beads placed in your natural hair. The beads are micro-sized and sit flat against the scalp for maximum comfort.

Because the wefts are sewn onto beaded rows rather than glued or fused, they lay incredibly flat and distribute weight evenly. This makes them ideal for fine to medium hair types that need gentle, lightweight extensions.

Hand-sewn wefts offer excellent volume with minimal bulk. Application takes 2-3 hours. They typically last 6-8 weeks before requiring a move-up, where beads are repositioned and wefts are re-sewn.
