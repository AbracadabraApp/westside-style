ATTACHMENT STYLE

Keratin Bonds: Individual strands fused one-by-one. Each bond is about the size of a grain of rice, color-matched, and placed strategically throughout your head for seamless integration.

Hand-Tied Wefts: Thin wefts sewn onto beaded rows. The rows sit horizontally across your head, completely flat and invisible even when you pull your hair up.

Tape-Ins: Flat panels sandwiched around small sections of hair. Each panel is about 1.5 inches wide and attaches using clear, flexible tape that disappears against your scalp.

MOVEMENT & FEEL

Keratin Bonds: Most natural movement. Each strand moves independently, creating realistic flow and bounce. The bonds are barely noticeable when you run your hands through your hair.

Hand-Tied Wefts: Natural, flexible movement. The wefts move with your scalp and don't create stiffness or restriction. Hair flows freely and feels light.

Tape-Ins: Smooth, lightweight movement. The panels are so thin you won't feel them. Hair swings and bounces naturally without any pulling or weight.

VISIBILITY IN UPDOS

Keratin Bonds: Individual bonds stay hidden in ponytails, braids, and buns. Because they're small and strategically placed, they remain undetectable even in pulled-back styles.

Hand-Tied Wefts: Specifically designed to stay invisible in updos. The flat rows don't create bumps or lines, so you can wear any style confidently.

Tape-Ins: Stay hidden when styled carefully. Position ponytails between panel rows or slightly lower to maintain coverage and avoid visibility.

BULK & VOLUME

Keratin Bonds: No added bulk. Individual bonds integrate seamlessly without creating thickness at the roots. Perfect for adding length without changing your natural volume profile.

Hand-Tied Wefts: Minimal bulk despite adding significant volume. The thin wefts create fullness without the heavy, thick feeling of traditional sew-ins.

Tape-Ins: Extremely lightweight. The panels are so thin they add length and volume without any bulky feeling or visible lines.
