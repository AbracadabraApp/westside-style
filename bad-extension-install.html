FIXING PROBLEMS

WHAT TO DO IF YOU'VE HAD A BAD INSTALL

Get them removed safely, let your hair recover, and find a qualified specialist.

SIGNS OF A BAD INSTALLATION

You're experiencing headaches or scalp pain from tension on your natural hair.

The extensions are visible — you can see bonds, tape, or wefts when your hair is down.

Your natural hair is breaking or thinning where the extensions are attached.

The extensions tangle constantly, even with regular brushing and care.

They're slipping out within days or weeks of installation.

IMMEDIATE STEPS

Don't wait. If you're in pain or seeing damage, get the extensions removed as soon as possible. Leaving them in causes more harm.

Don't remove them yourself. Cutting, pulling, or trying DIY removal can rip out your natural hair and cause permanent damage. You need a professional removal.

Find a specialist for removal. Even if you don't plan to reinstall, have someone experienced remove them properly. This protects your natural hair from further damage.

Document everything. Take photos of the damage, save receipts, and keep records if you need to request a refund or correction from the original stylist.

RECOVERY AND NEXT STEPS

After removal, give your hair a break. Use deep conditioning treatments and focus on repairing any damage before considering new extensions.

If your natural hair is weak, thin, or broken, it may need weeks or months to recover before it's ready to support extensions again.

When you're ready to try again, work with a specialist who focuses exclusively on extensions — not someone who does them occasionally between other services.

WHAT WENT WRONG

Most bad installations happen when stylists lack experience or try to rush the process. Extensions require precision and time.

Common mistakes include overloading sections with too much weight, placing bonds or wefts too close to the scalp, and choosing the wrong method for your hair type.

Sometimes low-quality hair is to blame — cheap extensions tangle, shed, and don't last. A specialist uses premium hair and knows how to assess quality.

HOW TO AVOID IT NEXT TIME

Ask about training and experience. How long has the stylist been installing extensions? How often do they do them?

Look at their portfolio. Do the before-and-after photos show natural, seamless results?

Read reviews carefully. Pay attention to mentions of damage, pain, or visibility issues.

Trust your instincts. If something feels rushed or the consultation is minimal, that's a red flag.
