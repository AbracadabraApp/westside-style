WHAT ARE TAPE-IN EXTENSIONS?

Quick application, easy maintenance, natural blend

Tape-in extensions consist of pre-taped wefts that are sandwiched between thin sections of your natural hair. The medical-grade adhesive tape creates a secure, flat bond that's nearly invisible and very comfortable.

Each tape-in panel is thin and lightweight, allowing them to blend naturally with your natural hair. The flat application means no bumps or ridges — just smooth, natural-looking volume and length.

Tape-ins are ideal for adding volume and length quickly. Application takes 45-90 minutes. They typically last 6-8 weeks before requiring a move-up, where the tapes are replaced and the extensions are repositioned.
