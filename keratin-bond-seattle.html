WHAT ARE KERATIN BOND EXTENSIONS?

Individual strand fusion for the most natural movement, feel, and longevity

Keratin bond extensions are the gold standard in hair extension methods. Each individual extension strand is fused to your natural hair using a protein-based keratin bond that's heated and molded around small sections of your hair.

This creates tiny, nearly invisible bonds that move naturally with your hair. Unlike wefts or tape-ins, keratin bonds offer unparalleled flexibility and the most natural look and feel — as if the extensions grew from your own scalp.

With proper care and maintenance, keratin bond extensions can last 4-6 months before requiring a move-up or replacement, making them an excellent long-term investment in your hair.
