WHAT ARE HAND TIED EXTENSIONS?

Hand tied extensions use high-quality wefts that are hand-sewn onto small, micro-beaded rows installed in your natural hair. This method creates an incredibly flat, lightweight application that's virtually undetectable.

The beaded rows distribute weight evenly across your scalp, making them especially comfortable for those with fine to medium hair density. Unlike traditional sew-in wefts, hand tied extensions lay flush against your head without bulk or discomfort.

Hand tied extensions typically last 6-8 weeks before requiring a move-up appointment, where the rows are re-tightened and repositioned as your natural hair grows. With proper maintenance, the same wefts can be reused multiple times.
