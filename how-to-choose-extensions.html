DECISION GUIDE

HOW DO I CHOOSE THE RIGHT METHOD?

Match your hair type, lifestyle, and maintenance preferences to the right extension method.

START WITH YOUR HAIR TYPE

Fine or thin hair: Hand-tied wefts are specifically designed for fine hair. They're lightweight, lay completely flat, and won't create bulk or stress on delicate strands. Tape-ins are also gentle and work well for fine hair. Keratin bonds can work but may be less ideal if your hair is very fine.

Medium to thick hair: All three methods work beautifully. Keratin bonds offer the most natural movement and longest wear time. Hand-tied wefts add volume without weight. Tape-ins provide fast installation and easy maintenance.

Very thick or coarse hair: Keratin bonds and hand-tied wefts handle thick hair best. Tape-ins can work but may require more panels for adequate coverage, which adds to the cost.

CONSIDER YOUR LIFESTYLE

Low-maintenance, set-and-forget: Choose keratin bonds. They last 4-6 months with no maintenance appointments and minimal daily care. Perfect if you don't want to think about your extensions.

Regular salon visits are fine: Hand-tied wefts or tape-ins work well. Both require move-ups or re-tapes every 6-8 weeks, but these appointments are quick (1-2 hours) and easy to schedule.

Love to style your hair frequently: All methods allow heat styling and updos. Keratin bonds and hand-tied wefts offer the most versatility. Tape-ins require careful positioning for ponytails but still allow full styling.

Product-heavy routine or oily scalp: Avoid tape-ins. The adhesive breaks down with oil-based products. Keratin bonds or hand-tied wefts are better choices if you can't give up your favorite hair oils.

THINK ABOUT TIME & BUDGET

Quick installation: Tape-ins take 1-2 hours. Hand-tied wefts take 2-3 hours. Keratin bonds take 3-5 hours or more. If you can't commit to a long appointment, tape-ins are your fastest option.

Lowest maintenance cost over time: Keratin bonds require the fewest appointments (one every 4-6 months). Hand-tied wefts and tape-ins need appointments every 6-8 weeks, which adds up over a year.

Hair longevity: Hand-tied weft hair lasts 1 year or more with proper care, reused at every move-up. Keratin bond hair can be reused 2-3 times if still in good condition. Tape-in hair typically lasts 6-8 months before replacement.

Lower commitment: Tape-ins are easiest to remove if you want to take a break or try something different. Keratin bonds and hand-tied wefts are longer-term commitments.

QUICK DECISION FRAMEWORK

CHOOSE KERATIN BONDS IF:

• You have medium to thick hair

• You want the longest wear time (4-6 months)

• You prefer minimal maintenance and fewer appointments

• You want the most natural movement and feel

• You're okay with a longer installation (3-5 hours)

CHOOSE HAND-TIED WEFTS IF:

• You have fine to thick hair (most versatile)

• You want volume without bulk or weight

• You don't mind move-up appointments every 6-8 weeks

• You want extensions that are invisible in updos

• You want hair that lasts 1 year or more

CHOOSE TAPE-INS IF:

• You want the fastest installation (1-2 hours)

• You have fine to medium hair

• You don't mind avoiding oil-based products

• You're okay with re-tape appointments every 6-8 weeks

• You want a lower-commitment option that's easy to remove
