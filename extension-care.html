HOW DO I CARE FOR MY EXTENSIONS?

Extensions need gentle care, but the routine is simple.

BRUSHING

Brush twice a day — morning and before bed. Use a loop brush or soft-bristle brush designed for extensions.

Start at the ends and work your way up. Never brush from the roots down, as this pulls on the bonds or attachment points.

Hold the hair at mid-length while brushing the ends to reduce tension on the attachments.

✦

WASHING

Wash 2-3 times per week. You don't need to wash daily — extensions don't get oily as quickly as natural hair.

Use sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner. Sulfates can weaken bonds and dry out extension hair.

Be gentle at the roots. Shampoo the scalp carefully without scrubbing or pulling at the attachment points. Wash the length of the hair normally.

Condition mid-length to ends only. Avoid applying conditioner near the roots or attachment points, as it can cause slipping.

✦

DRYING

Gently squeeze excess water out with a towel. Don't rub or twist — this causes tangling.

Let air dry when possible. If blow-drying, use low to medium heat and keep the dryer moving.

Never go to bed with wet extensions. They tangle easily when wet, and the bonds can weaken.

✦

STYLING

You can use heat tools (flat iron, curling iron) on extensions. Use a heat protectant spray first.

Keep heat tools away from the bonds or attachment points. Focus on styling the length of the hair.

Avoid heavy oils and serums near the roots — they can loosen tape or cause slippage.

✦

WHAT TO AVOID

Products with sulfates or alcohol — these dry out the hair and weaken bonds.

Sleeping with wet hair — leads to tangling and matting.

Tight ponytails or buns — constant tension can pull on attachment points.

Chlorine and salt water without rinsing — rinse immediately after swimming.
