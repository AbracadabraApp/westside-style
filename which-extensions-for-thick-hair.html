WHICH EXTENSIONS ARE BEST FOR THICK HAIR?

All methods work — your choice depends on lifestyle, budget, and desired longevity.

WHY THICK HAIR HAS MORE OPTIONS

Thick hair has strong, dense strands that can support more weight and handle different attachment methods without strain.

This means you can choose based on the result you want rather than being limited by hair strength concerns.

The main considerations become maintenance schedule, budget, and how you want the extensions to move and feel.

KERATIN BOND EXTENSIONS

Individual strand fusion creates the most natural movement. Each bond moves independently, so the hair flows and behaves like your natural hair.

They last 4-6 months before removal — the longest wear time of any method. You can reuse the hair multiple times if it's still in good condition.

Best for thick hair that can support the weight of hundreds of individual bonds without tension. The result is seamless and undetectable.

HAND-TIED EXTENSIONS

Thin wefts sewn onto beaded rows create volume and length with less bulk than traditional sew-ins. They lay flat and blend naturally.

Move-ups every 6-8 weeks keep them looking fresh as your hair grows. The hair itself lasts a year or longer with proper care.

Thick hair can handle multiple rows if you want significant length or fullness. The wefts distribute weight evenly across the scalp.

TAPE-IN EXTENSIONS

Lightweight sandwiched panels attach quickly and lay completely flat. Installation takes about an hour, and move-ups are fast.

They're gentle even on thick hair and blend seamlessly. The hair can be reused for 6-8 months before replacement.

Lower maintenance commitment than keratin bonds but still require move-ups every 6-8 weeks to stay secure and look natural.
