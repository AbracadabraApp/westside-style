KERATIN BOND EXTENSIONS

WHAT DO KERATIN TIPS LOOK LIKE ONCE THEY'RE IN?

Nearly invisible — the bonds blend naturally and move like your natural hair.

NATURAL AND UNDETECTABLE

Keratin bonds are small, flat, and color-matched to blend with your natural hair. Once installed, they're almost impossible to see or feel.

Each bond attaches an individual strand of extension hair to a small section of your natural hair. The result is hair that moves independently and naturally — not bulky or stiff.

When installed correctly, you can run your fingers through your hair without feeling the bonds. They sit flat against the scalp and don't create bumps or lumps.

HOW THEY BLEND

The bonds are placed strategically throughout your head, following your natural hair growth pattern. This creates natural integration from root to tip.

Because each bond is individual, the hair flows and behaves exactly like your own. There are no tracks or wefts that can shift or become visible.

The extension hair itself is high-quality human hair matched to your color and texture. It reflects light and moves just like natural hair.

WHAT YOU'LL NOTICE

Immediate length and fullness. You'll leave the salon with the hair you've been wanting — no growing out, no waiting.

Natural movement. The hair swings, bounces, and falls naturally. No one will be able to tell where your natural hair ends and the extensions begin.

Versatile styling. You can wear your hair up in ponytails, braids, or buns without worrying about the bonds showing. They're small and placed to stay hidden.

Comfortable wear. After the first day or two, you won't notice they're there. No tension, no pulling — just fuller, longer hair.

WHAT TO EXPECT OVER TIME

For the first few weeks, keratin bonds remain perfectly seamless and invisible. The hair looks freshly styled every day.

As your natural hair grows (about 4-6 months), the bonds will grow down with it. They remain secure and invisible, but eventually the growth creates enough distance that it's time for removal.

The extension hair itself holds up beautifully throughout the wear period. With proper care, it continues to look shiny, healthy, and natural until removal.
