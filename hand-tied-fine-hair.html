HAND-TIED FOR FINE HAIR

CAN I WEAR THEM WITH FINE OR THIN HAIR?

Absolutely — hand-tied wefts are specifically designed for fine hair.

WHY HAND-TIED WORKS FOR FINE HAIR

Hand-tied wefts were developed specifically to solve the problem of extensions for fine hair. Traditional sew-ins were too bulky and heavy — hand-tied changed that.

The wefts are incredibly thin and lightweight. Each strand is hand-tied individually, creating a soft, flexible weft that lays flat against your scalp without adding bulk.

The beaded rows distribute weight evenly across your entire head, rather than concentrating it in small sections. This prevents strain, breakage, and damage to delicate hair.

WHAT MAKES THEM DIFFERENT

No tension or pulling. The rows sit flat and gentle against your scalp. You won't feel headaches or discomfort from weight pulling on your natural hair.

Customizable coverage. Fine hair can start with 2-3 rows for natural fullness. If your hair handles it well, you can add more at your next move-up.

Completely hidden. Even with fine hair that doesn't provide much coverage, the rows stay invisible. They're designed to blend seamlessly even in updos.

Volume without weight. You get fullness and length without feeling like you're carrying extra hair. The wefts are light and barely noticeable.

HOW WE CUSTOMIZE FOR FINE HAIR

During your consultation, we assess your hair density, strand thickness, and scalp sensitivity to determine the right approach.

Fine hair typically starts with fewer rows — often 2-3 instead of 4-5. This creates natural-looking fullness without overwhelming your natural hair.

We use lighter hair if needed. Not every fine-haired client needs a full head of heavy hair — sometimes less is more for a natural result.

Placement matters. We position rows strategically to provide maximum volume where you need it while keeping everything comfortable and secure.

WHAT TO EXPECT

After installation, you'll notice immediate fullness and length without feeling weighed down. The wefts feel natural and barely there.

No breakage or damage when maintained properly. Regular move-ups and gentle care keep both your extensions and natural hair healthy.

You can style your hair normally — ponytails, braids, updos — without worrying about the rows showing or feeling uncomfortable.

Most fine-haired clients are thrilled with hand-tied wefts. They finally get the volume they've always wanted without sacrificing hair health.
