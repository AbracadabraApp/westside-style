WHAT ARE HAND-SEWN WEFT EXTENSIONS?

Flat wefts sewn onto micro-beaded rows — best for fine to medium hair

Hand-sewn weft extensions use wefts that are sewn onto micro-beaded rows in your natural hair. The beads are about 2mm thick, so they sit very flat and are hard to see or feel.

The beaded rows distribute weight evenly across your scalp, making them especially comfortable for those with fine to medium hair density. Unlike traditional sew-in wefts, hand-sewn wefts lay flush against your head without bulk or discomfort.

You'll need move-ups every 6-8 weeks (about $200-300 for tightening and repositioning). The wefts themselves can be reused 3-4 times before you need new hair — typically 9-12 months total.

WHY PEOPLE CHOOSE HAND TIED ROWS

BEST FOR FINE HAIR

REUSABLE WEFTS

High-quality wefts can be moved up and reused multiple times, making them cost-effective long-term.

FASTER INSTALL

90-120 minutes vs 3-5 hours for keratin bonds. Good if you can't sit for long appointments.
