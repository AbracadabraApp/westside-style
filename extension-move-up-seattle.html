WHAT IS A MOVE-UP?

As your natural hair grows, your extensions grow out with it. A move-up appointment repositions your existing extensions closer to your scalp, maintaining the seamless look and comfort you had when they were first installed.

During a move-up, we carefully remove your extensions, assess the health of your natural hair, and reinstall them in their proper position. For tape-ins and hand-tied rows, we replace the adhesive or re-tie the beads. For keratin bonds, we may reapply or add new bonds as needed.

Regular move-up appointments are essential for maintaining beautiful extensions. They prevent matting, tangling, and damage while keeping your extensions looking fresh and natural.

HOW OFTEN DO I NEED A MOVE-UP?

TAPE-IN

Every 6-8 weeks. Quick service with tape replacement.

HAND TIED

Every 6-8 weeks. Rows are re-tightened and repositioned.

KERATIN BOND

Every 8-12 weeks, depending on growth rate and care.

Pro tip: Book your next move-up appointment before you leave. Regular maintenance is the key to healthy, long-lasting extensions.

WHAT TO EXPECT

ASSESSMENT

We'll examine your extensions and natural hair health, checking for any needed adjustments or repairs.

REMOVAL & CLEANING

Extensions are carefully removed, and your natural hair is gently cleansed and detangled.

REINSTALLATION

Extensions are repositioned at the proper distance from your scalp using fresh adhesive, new beads, or reapplied bonds.

STYLING & CARE REVIEW

We'll finish with styling and review any care tips to keep your extensions looking their best.
