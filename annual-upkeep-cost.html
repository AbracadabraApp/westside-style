WHAT'S THE UPKEEP COST OVER A YEAR?

Compare total annual costs including installation, maintenance, and upkeep.

KERATIN BOND EXTENSIONS

Initial installation: Higher upfront cost due to time-intensive application (3-5 hours) and individual strand work.

Maintenance over a year: Minimal. Keratin bonds last 4-6 months, so you'll need 2-3 removal and reinstallation appointments per year. No maintenance appointments in between.

Hair replacement: The hair can be reused 2-3 times if still in good condition, which spreads the hair cost over multiple installations.

Product costs: Standard sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner. No special products required.

Annual total: Lower total cost over time due to fewer appointments and reusable hair, despite higher initial investment.

HAND-TIED WEFT EXTENSIONS

Initial installation: Moderate upfront cost. Installation takes 2-3 hours and includes custom weft sewing.

Maintenance over a year: Move-up appointments every 6-8 weeks. That's 6-8 appointments per year at a maintenance rate. These appointments are quick (1-2 hours) but add up over time.

Hair replacement: The weft hair lasts 1 year or more with proper care and is reused at every move-up. After a year, you'll need to invest in new hair.

Product costs: Standard sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner. No special restrictions.

Annual total: Higher total cost over a year due to frequent move-up appointments, but the hair longevity (1+ year) offsets some of the maintenance costs.

TAPE-IN EXTENSIONS

Initial installation: Lower to moderate upfront cost. Installation is fast (1-2 hours) and straightforward.

Maintenance over a year: Re-tape appointments every 6-8 weeks. That's 6-8 appointments per year. These appointments are quick (1-1.5 hours) and cost less than full installations.

Hair replacement: The hair typically lasts 6-8 months before needing replacement. You'll need to purchase new hair once or twice per year.

Product costs: Must use oil-free products. This may require switching to specialized extension-safe shampoos and conditioners, which can cost more than standard products.

Annual total: Moderate total cost. Frequent maintenance appointments and hair replacement add up, but lower initial investment and quick appointments keep it accessible.

COST FACTORS TO CONSIDER

Time is money. Keratin bonds require fewer appointments, which saves you travel time and scheduling hassle. Hand-tied wefts and tape-ins require appointments every 6-8 weeks, which means more time spent at the salon.

Hair longevity matters. Hand-tied weft hair lasts the longest (1+ year). Keratin bond hair can be reused 2-3 times. Tape-in hair needs replacement every 6-8 months. Factor in how often you'll need to buy new hair.

Product costs add up. If you use tape-ins, you'll need to switch to oil-free products exclusively. This can add $20-50 per month compared to standard products you may already own.

Your lifestyle affects cost. If you're rough on your hair, style frequently with heat, or swim regularly, your extensions may not last as long — increasing replacement costs across all methods.

WHICH IS MOST COST-EFFECTIVE?

Lowest cost per year: Keratin bonds typically have the lowest total annual cost due to fewer appointments and reusable hair, despite the higher initial investment.

Most predictable budget: Hand-tied wefts offer predictable costs with regular move-ups and long-lasting hair. You'll know exactly what to budget every 6-8 weeks.

Lowest commitment: Tape-ins have the lowest initial cost and are easiest to remove if you want to take a break, making them the most flexible option financially.

Best value for your goals: The "best" cost depends on what matters most to you. If you want to minimize salon visits and long-term cost, choose keratin bonds. If you want consistent, manageable appointments and maximum hair longevity, choose hand-tied wefts. If you want fast installation and flexibility, choose tape-ins.
