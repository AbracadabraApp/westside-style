WHAT HAPPENS IN A CONSULTATION?

WE TALK ABOUT YOUR GOALS

What do you want your hair to do for you? More length, more volume, easier styling? We start by understanding what you're looking for.

HAIR ASSESSMENT

I'll examine your natural hair — texture, density, length, and health — to determine which extension method will work best and look most natural.

METHOD RECOMMENDATION

I'll explain which method (keratin bond, hand tied, or tape-in) suits your hair type, lifestyle, and goals — and why. You'll get honest guidance, not a sales pitch.

PRICING & TIMELINE

We'll discuss pricing, maintenance schedule, and what to expect. No surprises, no hidden costs.

QUESTIONS & NEXT STEPS

Ask anything you want. If you're ready to move forward, we'll schedule your installation. If you need time to think, that's completely fine too.

WHY IS IT FREE?

Extensions are an investment. You should feel confident about the method, the cost, and the person doing the work before you commit.

I offer free consultations because I want you to make the right decision for your hair — whether that means booking with me or not. My goal is to give you honest, expert guidance with zero pressure.
