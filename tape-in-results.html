TAPE-IN EXTENSIONS

WHAT DO TAPE-INS LOOK LIKE ONCE THEY'RE IN?

Lightweight and invisible — the panels lay completely flat and blend beautifully.

THIN AND UNDETECTABLE

Tape-in extensions use ultra-thin adhesive strips that sandwich a small section of your natural hair between two panels of extension hair.

Each panel is about 1.5 inches wide and lies completely flat against your head. The tape itself is clear and flexible, so there's no visible line or bump.

Once installed, you can't feel the panels when you run your hands through your hair. They move naturally with your scalp and don't create any texture or resistance.

HOW THEY BLEND

The panels are placed strategically around your head, following your natural hair sections. This creates even coverage from roots to ends.

Your natural hair covers the top panels, hiding the attachment points. Even when you brush your hair back or wear it in different styles, the tapes remain invisible.

The extension hair is matched perfectly to your color and texture, so it blends seamlessly with your natural hair. Light, movement, and texture all match.

WHAT YOU'LL NOTICE

Instant transformation. Tape-ins install quickly, and you'll leave with immediate length and fullness. No waiting period — just walk out with the hair you want.

Lightweight feel. Despite adding significant volume, tape-ins feel incredibly light. There's no heavy pull or tension on your scalp.

Natural movement. The hair swings and flows naturally. The panels are flexible and move with your head, creating realistic motion and bounce.

Comfortable daily wear. After the first day, you won't notice they're there. They don't shift, slip, or create discomfort throughout the day.

WHAT TO EXPECT OVER TIME

For the first 6-8 weeks, tape-ins stay secure and perfectly flat. Your hair looks salon-fresh with minimal styling effort.

As your natural hair grows, the taped panels grow down with it. Eventually, the growth creates enough distance that it's time for a re-tape appointment.

At your re-tape, the old adhesive is removed and replaced with fresh tape. The same extension hair is reused, so you're just refreshing the attachment — not starting over.

The extension hair itself holds up beautifully between re-tapes. With proper care, it stays shiny, smooth, and natural-looking throughout the wear period.
