HAND-TIED EXTENSIONS

WHAT DO HAND-SEWN WEFTS LOOK LIKE ONCE THEY'RE IN?

Incredibly flat and undetectable — the wefts blend seamlessly even in updos.

FLAT AND SEAMLESS

Hand-tied wefts are sewn onto beaded rows that sit completely flat against your scalp. There's no bulk or ridge — just smooth, even coverage.

The wefts themselves are incredibly thin. Hand-tied construction means each strand is individually secured, creating a soft, flexible weft that conforms to your head shape.

Once installed, you can run your fingers along your scalp and barely feel the rows. The beads are tiny and covered by the weft, making them invisible even to touch.

✦

HOW THEY BLEND

The beaded rows follow your natural hair growth pattern, creating horizontal lines of support that distribute weight evenly across your scalp.

Your natural hair covers the attachment points. Even when you pull your hair back, the rows remain hidden beneath your top layer of hair.

The extension hair itself blends perfectly with your natural color and texture. It moves, reflects light, and behaves exactly like your own hair.

✦

WHAT YOU'LL NOTICE

Instant volume and length. The wefts add fullness without weight, creating hair that looks thick and healthy but doesn't feel heavy.

Natural movement. Because the wefts are so thin and flexible, your hair flows and bounces naturally. No stiffness or bulkiness.

Versatile styling. You can wear ponytails, braids, and half-updos without worrying about rows showing. They're designed to stay hidden in all styles.

Comfortable all day. After a day or two of adjustment, you won't remember they're there. No tension, no pulling — just fuller hair.

✦

WHAT TO EXPECT OVER TIME

For the first 6-8 weeks, hand-tied wefts stay perfectly flat and secure. Your hair looks salon-fresh every day with minimal effort.

As your natural hair grows, the beaded rows start to grow down away from your scalp. This is normal and expected — it's why you need move-up appointments.

At your move-up, the beads are moved back up to the scalp and the wefts are re-sewn. The same hair is reused, so you're not starting from scratch — just repositioning for continued seamless wear.
