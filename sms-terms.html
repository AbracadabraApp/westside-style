SMS LEAD ALERTS TERMS

Last updated: October 2026

PROGRAM

Westside Style Lead Alerts is a text message program operated by Josh Petersen for Westside Style, a hair extension salon in Seattle, WA. It sends new-inquiry notifications to Westside Style staff who sign up at westside.style/lead-alerts.html. Each alert contains the details a customer submitted on westside.style or the phone number of a customer who called Westside Style, so staff can follow up.

FREQUENCY AND COST

Message frequency varies; you receive one message per new customer inquiry, typically 1 to 10 messages per week. Message and data rates may apply.

HELP AND OPT-OUT

Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to opt out at any time; you will receive one final message confirming you are unsubscribed and no further messages will be sent. Reply START to resubscribe.
