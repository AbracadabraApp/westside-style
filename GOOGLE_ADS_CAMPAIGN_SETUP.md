### Step-by-Step: Create Conversion Actions

#### 1. Log into Google Ads
- Go to ads.google.com
- Click **Tools & Settings** (wrench icon)
- Under **Measurement**, click **Conversions**

#### 2. Create New Conversion Action
- Click the blue **+ New Conversion Action** button
- Select **Website**

#### 3. Configure Conversion for "Keratin Bond Lead"

**Conversion Details:**
- **Goal:** Submit lead form
- **Conversion Name:** Keratin Bond Lead
- **Category:** Submit lead form
- **Value:** $1,200 (use different value for each conversion)
- **Count:** Every conversion
- **Conversion Window:** 30 days
- **Attribution Model:** Data-driven (or Last click)

**Tag Setup:**
- Choose: "Use Google tag manager, Google Analytics, or other website builder"
- Copy the conversion label (looks like: `AbC1dE2fG3HiJk`)

#### 4. Repeat for Each Service
Create 3 separate conversion actions:

| Conversion Name | Value | Conversion Label (you'll generate) |
|----------------|-------|-----------------------------------|

## Local Service Ads

### What Are Google Local Service Ads (LSAs)?

Local Service Ads (formerly Google Guaranteed) appear ABOVE regular Google Ads with:
- "Google Screened" or "Google Guaranteed" badge
- Pay-per-lead pricing (not per click)
- Prominent placement with phone number
- Higher trust factor

### Should Westside Style Apply?

**Pros:**
- Only pay for actual phone calls/messages
- Appear above regular ads
- Google screens your business (builds trust)
- Average cost per lead often lower than Google Ads
- Great for local businesses

**Cons:**
- Beauty/hair services eligibility varies by location
- Requires background check ($50-100)
- Less control over ad copy
- Seattle market may have limited availability for hair salons

### How to Check Eligibility & Apply

1. Go to: https://ads.google.com/local-services-ads/
2. Enter business type: "Beauty Salon" or "Hair Salon"
3. Enter location: Seattle, WA
4. Check if hair extensions services are available

**Requirements if eligible:**
- Business license (cosmetology license)
- Insurance (general liability)
- Background check for Jennifer
- Google Business Profile (required)

### Recommendation

**Try it if:**
- Available for hair salons in Seattle
- You have required licenses/insurance
- Budget allows $200-500/month for LSAs

## Quick Reference

### Key Metrics to Track

| Metric | What It Means | Target |
|--------|--------------|--------|
| CTR (Click-Through Rate) | % of people who click your ad | 5-10% |
| CPC (Cost Per Click) | Average cost when someone clicks | $2.50-$5.00 |
| Conversion Rate | % of clicks that become leads | 5-12% |
| CPA (Cost Per Acquisition) | Cost to get one lead | $80-$200 |
| Quality Score | Google's ad relevance rating | 7-10 |
| Impression Share | % of possible impressions you got | 60-80% |
