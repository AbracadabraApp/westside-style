WHAT ARE TAPE-IN EXTENSIONS?

Seamless, lightweight panels that blend beautifully and lay completely flat

Tape-in extensions consist of pre-taped wefts that are sandwiched between thin sections of your natural hair. The medical-grade adhesive tape creates a secure, flat bond that's virtually invisible and incredibly comfortable.

Each tape-in panel is thin and lightweight, allowing them to blend seamlessly with your natural hair. The flat application means no bumps or ridges — just smooth, natural-looking volume and length.

Tape-in extensions are ideal for adding volume and length quickly. They typically last 6-8 weeks before requiring a move-up, where the tapes are replaced and the extensions are repositioned. The wefts themselves can be reused multiple times with proper care.

WHY CHOOSE TAPE-IN EXTENSIONS?
