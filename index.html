Meet Jennifer Arges

Stylist & Owner

With over a decade of experience in luxury hair styling, Jennifer Arges founded Westside Style with a vision to create more than just beautiful hair – but to elevate confidence and celebrate individual beauty.

Specializing in extensions, color transformations, and Brazilian blowouts, Jennifer brings artistry and technical expertise to every appointment. Her passion for hair started at a young age and has evolved into a dedication to making every client feel their absolute best.

"I believe that great hair isn't just about the latest trends – it's about understanding each client's unique style and lifestyle. Every service is personalized because you are more than worth it."

- Jennifer Arges
